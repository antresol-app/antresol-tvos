Antresol brings your home media library to Apple TV. The films, TV shows and music you keep on your own computer or NAS, in one clean, native library on the big screen.

Antresol is a player for the Antresol home media server, which you run yourself. No content is provided: the app shows only the files you put on your server.

FILMS AND TV SHOWS
• Pick up where you left off, on any of your devices.
• Audio tracks, subtitles and a choice of quality.
• Seasons and episodes, with Up Next for the following episode.
• Want to Watch and Watched marks.

MUSIC
• Albums, artists, songs, genres and playlists.
• Favourites and synced lyrics.
• Background playback with the Siri Remote and Control Center.

FAMILY
• Profiles with passwords and kid profiles with age limits.

TOP SHELF
• Continue Watching and Recently Added right on the Apple TV Home screen.

PRIVACY
The app connects only to your server. The developer provides and sells no content and receives none of your data. There are no ads, analytics or tracking.

You need an Antresol server on your computer or NAS, version 0.3.0 or newer. Install guide: https://sidun-av.github.io/antresol/install.html

Only add media you have the rights to.
